Bring your SONOFF Zigbee devices into Homey: switches and dimmers for the wall and the junction box, smart plugs with energy monitoring, roller shutter and curtain controllers, thermostatic radiator valves, water valves and sensors for temperature, humidity, motion, presence, doors, windows and water leaks. SONOFF RF remotes and receivers on 433 MHz work too.

Use them in your Flows to switch lights and appliances, keep an eye on energy use, control heating and irrigation and respond to buttons and sensors. Options such as power-on behavior, inching, detach mode and calibration are right in the device settings.
